\begin{abstract}
Spatially targeted optostimulation is a powerful technique in systems neuroscience.
Many studies have spatially targeted light in behaving mice by shining a small light spot at the brain through the exposed skull.
Targeting was achieved using either manual positioning of a fibre optic or automatically positioning  light using movable mirrors. 
Despite the advantages of the latter approach, the technology remains hard to access because no easy to use tools have been built by the open hardware community.
Here we present `Zapit', a compact galvo-based point stimulator an associated easy to use, cross-platform, software interface. 

\keywords{Optogenetics, Neuroscience, Software Tool, Optics}
\end{abstract}
