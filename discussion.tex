\subsection{Comparison to other approaches}

There are other effective approaches for rapid programmatic photostimulation in head-fixed behavioural tasks: those based on , digital micro-mirror devices (DMDs) or spatial lights modulators (SLM).

DMD-based approaches can activate arbitrarily-shaped areas by imaging a movable micro-mirror array onto the sample (\cite{Chong2020ManipulatingPerception}).
However, since DMD devices will selectively deflect most of the produced light, a very powerful and expensive laser is needed to cover a large area, such as the whole mouse cortex. SLM-based solutions are notably useful in targeting neural tissue with cellular resolution (REF), but their capabilities (and associated cost) far exceed the functionality needed to produce light patterns during wide-scale cortical stimulation. Deflection of light beam by galvanometric scanners (galvos) to the target location are a cost-effective, and easy to implement method for this kind of experiments.
