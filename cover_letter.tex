\opening{Dear Editors,}

We write to re-submit our manuscript, \textit{Zapit: Open Source Laser-Scanning Photostimulation For Neuroscience}, for consideration as a Tools and Resources article in eLife.

In re-reading the manuscript, we agree with the editors' feedback: we failed to convey the impact and novelty of our approach.  We have now revised the manuscript to fully address this oversight, including both changes to the text, and an additional supplementary figure.

We acknowledge that laser-scanning approaches have been around for several years. However, the laboratories that pioneered these technically sophisticated methods have not released comprehensive implementation details. This is a major barrier to the adoption of this powerful technology, particularly for early career investigators. To our knowledge, Zapit is the only complete and accessible platform for laser-scanning optogenetics (we now include a table in the manuscript for reference). In addition to its democratizing impact, Zapit goes beyond the state-of-the-art and offers a unique solution for the field. The software is user-friendly, includes a general-purpose cross-platform API, and is supported by extensive documentation. The hardware includes CAD models, and its modular design facilitates a range of configurations, including different lasers and opsins.

The Zapit project is impactful because it empowers diverse research labs to embrace a technique perceived to be complex. The project is novel because it presents the first fully integrated software and modular hardware solution, extending functionality beyond that of any published system.

We believe eLife is the right place to publish democratizing, open-source tools (in line with others published by eLife, e.g. BonVision and CaImAn). We are therefore eager to submit a revised version of the manuscript that directly highlights these advances and the potential impact of Zapit as suggested by the reviewing editors.

\closing{Yours sincerely,}
